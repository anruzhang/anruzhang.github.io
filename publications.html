Research portfolio

Publications

My research spans the foundations and methodology of statistics and machine learning, with particular interests in high-dimensional inference, generative AI, tensor and low-rank learning, optimization, and computational complexity. In parallel, I develop statistical and machine-learning methods for complex scientific data, especially in health and biomedicine.

How to read this page

The bibliography remains chronological. Topic filters are provided as alternate views, so theoretical, methodological, computational, and scientific work can be explored without forcing papers into a single identity.

Research across the spectrum

Foundations → methods → scientific impact

Foundations

Statistical & ML theory

High-dimensional inference, minimax theory, concentration, phase transitions, computational limits, and learning theory.

Learning

Machine learning & generative AI

Diffusion and flow models, synthetic data, representation learning, transformers, and modern generative methodology.

Methods

Tensors, low rank & optimization

Tensor decomposition and regression, matrix methods, Riemannian optimization, functional data, and scalable algorithms.

Applications

Scientific & biomedical data

Electronic health records, genomics, single-cell and spatial omics, microbiome, neuroimaging, and other scientific domains.
